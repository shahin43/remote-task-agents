# Common Gaps

Things that are easy to miss before delivering a paper. Not a checklist — just patterns that often get overlooked. Skim this quickly; not every item applies to every paper.

---

## Formatting consistency

- Heading hierarchy: H1 once (title only), H2 for sections, H3 for subsections — no skipped levels
- Tables have header rows; summary/total rows in bold
- Financial notation is consistent throughout (currency symbol + amount + suffix, e.g. "£150k")
- System names and technical identifiers in `monospace`
- ALL CAPS not used for emphasis

## Data credibility

- Financial figures include context (vs budget, vs prior year, vs target, or vs benchmark)
- Assumptions listed explicitly — not buried in prose
- Data sources cited or methodology described
- Risks have mitigations or are explicitly marked as accepted
- Active voice preferred — "We recommend X" not "It is recommended that X"
