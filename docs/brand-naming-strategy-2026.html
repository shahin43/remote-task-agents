The Relay8 GRID

Use the four objectives as a product doctrine. GRID is memorable, operational, and broad enough to guide architecture, positioning, roadmap decisions, and customer proof.

Governed by policy

Identity, scope, approvals, budgets, and external effects are controlled by the platform—not improvised by the model.

Reliable by design

Isolated attempts, durable state, idempotent commands, recovery, verification, and explicit terminal outcomes.

Integrated across the work

Agents, models, repositories, tools, channels, sandboxes, and customer workflows connect through typed seams.

Delivery measured by outcomes

Track accepted deliverables, quality, rework, review time, duration, tokens, cost, policy decisions, and business value.

Reliable execution. Measurable delivery.

Expanded line

Reliable by design. Governed by policy. Integrated for work. Measured by outcome.

Best for architecture, launch posts, README copy, and sales material.

Clearance before announcement

A web screen reduces obvious mistakes; it does not establish legal availability or ownership.

  1. Registrar and handle check: verify candidate domains, GitHub organization/repository, npm/PyPI/crates/container namespaces, and social handles.
  2. Trademark search: engage counsel to search UKIPO, EUIPO, USPTO, WIPO, Companies House and relevant common-law use—especially Nice Classes 9, 42 and possibly 35.
  3. Phonetic/confusion review: assess Relay8 alongside Relay, 8Relay hardware, Ennov8 and Enov8—not only exact spelling.
  4. Customer pronunciation test: show the name verbally to 10 target buyers; ask them to spell it, recall it a day later, and describe what they expect it does.
  5. Community package reservation: reserve namespaces only after legal review; publish a placeholder README and security contact before launch.
  6. Brand lock: approve word mark, logo mark, capitalization, tagline, edition naming, category descriptor, and migration from remote-sandbox-agents.
Recommended decision gate: shortlist Relay8, Ennov8 Relay, and Ennov8 Steward; complete legal/domain/package clearance; then choose one. If Relay8 clears, it is the strongest launch brand from this review.
